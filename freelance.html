1. What "AI freelancing" actually pays

The AI-training-and-evaluation market is real and hiring. Companies need humans to correct, rank, and judge model outputs. Indonesia is one of the most in-demand languages — most model output is English, so bilingual ID↔EN raters are scarce and paid accordingly.

Verified floor: The lowest tier (general annotation) pays $10-15/hr on Appen/Toloka. Specialized eval (coding, voice, agentic) pays $20-40/hr. This is not passive income — it's hourly contract work, paid weekly or biweekly.

5. Payout rails: getting USD → IDR

Two proven paths from Aldo's Airtm setup:

Path A — Airtm (for Outlier and USD-balance platforms):
  1. Outlier pays USD to Airtm (weekly, Tuesday)
  2. Airtm is your USD wallet — no US virtual account needed
  3. Cash out to DANA/OVO/GoPay or direct bank transfer (BCA etc.)
  4. Prefer direct bank transfer (guaranteed) over P2P peers
  5. If P2P: 4.9+ rating, 100+ completed, stay on-platform escrow, release only after IDR lands
Path B — Payoneer (for Appen/Toloka/Alignerr/Mindrift):
  1. Each platform pays USD to Payoneer
  2. Payoneer → IDR bank (5-7 days, ~2% FX fee) OR Payoneer card for direct spend
  3. Min withdrawal typically $10-50
Never go off-platform. Anyone DMing as "Airtm support" is a scam. Always transact in the official UI. Always release escrow only after funds settle.

6. The KYC gate (and how to clear it)

KYC is the real gate, not signup. Every platform above will let you create an account and queue tasks. But you cannot withdraw until you verify your identity. In Indonesia this is:

DocumentStatus
KTP (national ID)Required by all. Must be readable, not expired.
Selfie + KTP photoRequired by Airtm, Payoneer, Sribu.
NPWP (tax ID)Only needed above ~Rp60M/year revenue (Brunei/Airtm threshold). Skip until you hit it.
Bank account in your nameRequired for Sribu/Projects/Fastwork direct payout.

Action: Photograph your KTP clearly, front and back. Have the files ready. Clear KYC before you have money trapped in a wallet — once cash lands in an unverified Airtm, you can't access it until KYC is done.

8. Scams to avoid

Red flags:
  • Any platform asking for upfront payment to unlock tasks. Legit platforms pay you.
  • "Send crypto to verify your wallet" — never.
  • "Airtm support" DMing you on Telegram/WhatsApp — never. Use official app only.
  • P2P off-escrow ("just transfer directly, I'll send more tomorrow") — never.
  • Gumroad / Fiverr sellers offering "Outlier tasks for sale" — against Outlier ToS, account ban.
